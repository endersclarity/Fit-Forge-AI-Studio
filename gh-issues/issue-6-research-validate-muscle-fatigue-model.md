## Problem
FitForge has implemented intelligent muscle capacity learning but lacks documented research validating these models against exercise science literature.

## Solution
Conduct formal research sprint to:
- Literature review on muscle engagement percentages (EMG studies, biomechanics)
- Validate recovery curve against supercompensation theory
- Document mathematical foundation for baseline learning
- Assess confidence levels for each model component
- Identify gaps needing validation or user calibration
- Create comprehensive research findings document
